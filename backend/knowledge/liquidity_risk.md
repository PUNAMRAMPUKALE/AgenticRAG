# Liquidity Risk Disclosure — Horizon Core Equity Fund

The fund may invest up to 15% of net assets in illiquid securities. During stressed markets, redemptions may be delayed beyond the standard 30-day notice period if the board invokes an extraordinary liquidity gate.

A liquidity gate, if invoked, cannot exceed 10 business days in a rolling 90-day window.

This disclosure does not change the standard redemption notice period of 30 calendar days under normal market conditions.
