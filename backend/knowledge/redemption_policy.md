# Horizon Trust — Redemption Policy

Effective as of 31 March 2026. Jurisdiction: United States. Product: Horizon Core Equity Fund.

Investors may redeem shares of the Horizon Core Equity Fund by submitting a written redemption request to the transfer agent.

The **redemption notice period is 30 calendar days**. Requests received after the cutoff are treated as received on the next business day.

Redemptions are processed at the next available net asset value (NAV) after the notice period has elapsed. There is no redemption fee for holdings longer than 90 days. Early redemptions (held less than 90 days) may incur a 1.00% short-term trading fee.

Cutoff time for same-day receipt is 16:00 US Eastern Time.

Contact: transfer-agent@horizontrust.example
