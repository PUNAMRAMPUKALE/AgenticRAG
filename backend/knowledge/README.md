# Northstar Financial Group — Agentic RAG Business Knowledge Corpus

This corpus contains fictional company/business documents intended to serve as the **knowledge base consumed by an Agentic RAG system**. They are business reports, operating procedures, risk reviews, incident records, governance documents and structured datasets — not explanations of RAG.

The PDF reports are long-form business artifacts; the Excel workbooks provide structured records for exact-number questions.

All company facts, figures, decisions, names and metrics are synthetic. Public regulatory obligations must be sourced separately from official regulators and licensed sources.
